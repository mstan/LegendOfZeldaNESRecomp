#Zelda: Remastered 1.3#
-Many thanks to KYA for extensive support in developing this hack. Also, many thanks to Sour for creating the Mesen emulator. Lastly, special thanks to ShadowOne333 for assistance in incorporating aspects of Zelda 1 Redux in this pack.
-This pack provides enhanced graphics, music, and sound effects for the NES game, The Legend of Zelda. It should be used with a version of the Mesen NES emulator that can support layered backgrounds.
-To summarize, this is a hack that builds upon other hacks. I do not claim credit for some of the work many others have done in prior hacks that have helped make this possible.
-I also do not claim credit for the Mesen emulator that allows this HD pack to work. Read the credits section for more information on the many individuals who have helped make this possible.
-ALSO, SUPPORT NINTENDO BY BUYING OFFICIAL NINTENDO PRODUCTS!!! This is a fan made labor of love that is only intended to pay tribute to the Zelda series (like fan fiction, or fan art).
-Keep in mind, this pack is merely a MOD. This hack does not work as a game on its own. Think of it like a Minecraft texture pack.

#Getting Started#
-Obtain the necessary ROM file: You must supply your own legitimate North American ROM of The Legend of Zelda for NES. A copy of the necessary ROM will not be provided in this pack. I DO NOT SUPPORT PIRACY!
	-Failure to obtain a legitimate copy of the ROM may result in a swarm of burly FBI agents rappelling through your bedroom window.
	-The agents will likely seize your computer as well as your creepy anime figurine collection. Don't say I didn't warn you.

		!!THE PACK MUST BE USED WITH A ZELDA PRG0 ROM THAT HAS A SHA-1 CHECKSUM OF DAB79C84934F9AA5DB4E7DAD390E5D0C12443FA2!!
		!!If the checksum does not match exactly, the pack will not operate correctly!!
		!!Keep in mind there is another version of Zelda for NES (PRG1) which is not compatible with this pack!!
		!!The continue screen of PRG1 has the following warning at the continue screen. If your game has this warning at the continue screen, it is not the correct version!!
			"CAUTION  TO AVOID DAMAGING GAME INFO SAVED, HOLD IN RESET BUTTON AS YOU TURN POWER OFF."

-Download a version of the Mesen NES emulator that supports layered backgrounds. Mesen version 0.9.9.62 should be compatible.

		!!AT THE TIME OF THIS WRITING, THE PACK WILL ONLY WORK WITH A DEVELOPMENTAL VERSION OF MESEN. IT WILL NOT WORK WITH THE STABLE RELEASE 0.9.9 RELEASED ON FEB 1, 2020!!

-Open the Mesen emulator by clicking on "Mesen.exe" file.
-Navigate to "Options," then "Video," and ensure the box next to the "Use HDNes HD packs" is checked.
-In the same "Video" menu, I also suggest turning Vertical Sync on to ensure a smooth gameplay experience (this is optional).
-If you haven't already done so, navigate to "Options," then "Input" to configure your input settings. I recommend using a controller if possible.
-Navigate to "File," then "Open."
-Find your ROM of Zelda (must be PRG0) and double click on it to begin the game.
-Navigate to "Tools," then "Install HD Pack."
-Locate the "ZeldaRemastered.zip" file and double click on it.
-The emulator should prompt you to reset. When this occurs, select "OK."
-Press F11 if you wish to use Fullscreen mode.
-If you have completed the steps above correctly, the game should not be running with enhanced graphics and sound.
-Enjoy!
