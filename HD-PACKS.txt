Zelda Remastered and other Mesen HD packs

Open Mods, select Zelda Remastered HD, and choose Select file under Mesen HD
pack download. Choose the original ZIP you downloaded, enable the feature, and
click Play. Use your original USA PRG0 Zelda ROM; do not pre-patch it.

The shared importer handles the original outer download, a nested pack ZIP,
or hires.txt from an extracted pack. No Python or manual conversion is needed.
The support .nesmod contains integration rules only. All texture, audio and
patch files come from your own download and are imported into a private local
cache. Your download and original ROM remain unchanged. The original ZIP may
be removed after a successful import; keep the imported files under mods/imports.

Zelda Remastered HD Support is already installed and disabled by default.
The separate Zelda-Remastered-Support.nesmod is the same content-free recipe;
it requires v1.9.4 or later. Older locally converted packages can still work;
select support version 2.0.0 in Mods to use the direct-download workflow.

HD and Voxel display modes are mutually exclusive. Return to the launcher and
restart to change the selected pack. Disable HD to return to stock graphics.
Current HD support covers textures, layered backgrounds and the pack's IPS.
Replacement music/SFX remain unsupported; the original NES audio is used.

Pack creators' notices are retained in the local import. No Remastered art,
audio, IPS, or game ROM is included in this game or in the support .nesmod.
